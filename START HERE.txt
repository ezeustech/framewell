FRAMEWELL
Your PC. Your session.

1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click Framewell.exe.

No installer, terminal commands, account, or developer tools are needed to
open the dashboard. Keep Framewell.exe, its configuration file, and the app
folder together. Open the EXE from the extracted folder, not inside the ZIP.

Supports Windows 11 x64 or Windows 10 version 1903 and later with the standard
Windows PowerShell 5.1 and .NET Framework 4.8 components. The launcher explains
missing components or an incomplete extraction before trying to open the app.

Administrative tools request Windows approval only when you choose them.
This is an unsigned release candidate; Windows reputation checks still apply.

Your settings and profiles are stored in app\state. Keep that folder when
upgrading; do not share it publicly. The dashboard makes no changes just by
opening. Choose your apps and settings before starting a gaming session.
